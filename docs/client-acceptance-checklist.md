# Client acceptance checklist

22 October 2026. All items below are pending client acceptance unless explicitly recorded later. Passing automated tests does not approve content, policies or a public launch.

## Prototype review with fictional information

- [ ] Approve the public page order, navigation, responsive layouts and copy.
- [ ] Walk through application → pending member → admin approval → active membership scenario → eligible local preview; confirm suspension still removes eligibility.
- [ ] Find the eligible submitted business in the public directory, open its full profile, verify private contacts are excluded, then confirm refresh/reset removes the tab-only listing.
- [ ] Edit a profile and inspect its public-only preview; leave optional public identity blank and confirm private contacts are not substituted.
- [ ] Create/rename a category without changing existing IDs, associations or prices.
- [ ] Draft, review and publish a certification reference and resource; confirm drafts stay out of public views and refresh clears session changes.
- [ ] Try category Q&A, first-response moderation, reviews and free-phone request/assignment/member acceptance.
- [ ] Check featured eligibility and analytics definitions, including multi-category counting.
- [ ] Change a linked profile/application's featured entitlement and inspect the homepage in this tab; confirm suspension/payment failure removes its card while retaining entitlement.
- [ ] Try member simulated checkout, failed renewal/recovery, cancellation back/confirmation and returning checkout. Confirm $0 charged, no card inputs, one-category reference price and reset/reload clearing.
- [ ] Confirm keyboard/mobile behavior and the honest video/map/error fallbacks.
- [ ] Review the [owner walkthrough](admin-guide.md) and list requested revisions.

## Content and policy decisions

- [ ] Supply approved logo, About story/film/poster/captions/transcript, hero media, editorial articles, certification references, business photos and public locations/contacts.
- [ ] Approve public professional/owner name and email policy, support details, Privacy/Terms and disclaimer placement.
- [ ] Confirm credential/review verification and moderation rules; agree on truthful certification wording.
- [ ] Confirm Q&A response/publication/notification policy and free-call duration, matching, scheduling and cancellation responsibilities.
- [ ] Confirm membership start/grace/cancellation rules, multi-category billing and featured price/duration/ranking.
- [ ] Confirm location data, service areas, radius behavior and the production map method.

## Connected platform and launch acceptance

These require later scoped authorization and real service testing.

- [ ] Real accounts, server ownership/admin permissions, durable records and secure media handling pass access tests.
- [ ] Real billing events, cancellations/failures/recovery and independent listing eligibility work across sessions.
- [ ] Actual inquiry/Q&A/consultation/account notifications and newsletter consent/unsubscribe behavior are verified.
- [ ] Public content publication and operational reporting read authorized saved records.
- [ ] Production security, backups/recovery, monitoring, supported physical devices/browsers and agreed mobile performance are verified.
- [ ] Client domain/HTTPS, intended deployed revision, search visibility, final owner training and launch approval are recorded.

See [remaining work and inputs](../updates/remaining-work.md). No real services or public deployment are authorized by this checklist.
